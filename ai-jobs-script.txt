Is AI going to replace your job? Not in the future, boss... It has already started!

In 2026, the tech industry has already seen more than one lakh job cuts. Oracle, Amazon, Dell, Meta, Microsoft... And these are not small companies... They are Big Tech!

According to reports, in 2026, Oracle cut around twenty-one thousand jobs. Amazon cut around seventeen thousand. Dell cut around eleven thousand. Meta cut around ten thousand. And Microsoft cut around four thousand eight hundred jobs.

Recently, we are seeing the impact in India as well. PayPal India cut around two hundred and twenty employees. There have also been fresh layoff reports from Oracle India. Adidas has also reduced roles in its technology team in India.

But what is the reason? AI has arrived... but we can't simply say that humans are no longer needed. Companies are spending billions on AI. At the same time, they are restructuring teams and automating repetitive work.

So the real question is... Is AI going to replace your job? Maybe not every job. But think about this... If a developer can do five hours of work with AI in just one hour... Does a company still need the same-sized team?

So, the competition of the future is not humans versus AI. It is a person who doesn't use AI, versus a person who knows how to use AI effectively.

What do you think? Will AI create more jobs? Or will it eat away existing jobs?
