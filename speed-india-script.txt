How fast can you travel from Kashmir to Kanyakumari? The distance is roughly 3,500 kilometres.

By car, at around 80 to 90 kilometres per hour, it would take about 44 hours.

By train, at around 100 to 120 kilometres per hour, roughly 35 hours.

By flight, cruising at around 850 to 900 kilometres per hour, just about 4 hours.

But now, things get crazy!

An Indian Su 30 M K I fighter jet has a top speed around Mach 2, roughly 2,100 to 2,600 kilometres per hour. At that speed, Kashmir to Kanyakumari could take around 1 hour 20 minutes.

Now imagine the BrahMos supersonic cruise missile, travelling at around Mach 3, or roughly 3,500 kilometres per hour. That's only about 1 hour!

And then comes the monster...

A ballistic missile like Agni Five can reach speeds of around Mach 20 plus during its flight. At those speeds, we are talking about just a few minutes to cover thousands of kilometres.

From a car journey taking nearly two days, to a missile covering the same distance in minutes.

India's speed game is on another level!
