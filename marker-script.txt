Don't scroll. In two minutes, you'll understand one system design concept that shows up in almost every interview.

The concept is the load balancer. Think of it as the traffic cop of the internet.

Here's the problem. A single server simply can't handle millions of users at once.

The fix is simple. Spread the load, sending requests across many servers.

How does it choose a server? Three common strategies: round robin, least connections, and I P hash.

Here's the bonus. If one server dies, the load balancer automatically reroutes traffic to the healthy ones.
