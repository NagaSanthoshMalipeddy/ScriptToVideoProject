One of the most disputed regions in the world is Jammu and Kashmir.

But why is this region divided between India, Pakistan, and China?

Pakistan controls parts of the region that India refers to as Pakistan-occupied Jammu and Kashmir, including Azad Jammu and Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan.

India and Pakistan are separated here by the Line of Control, or LoC.

Then there's the Siachen Glacier.

In 1984, India launched Operation Meghdoot and established military positions in the Siachen region.

Now look towards the east.

Aksai Chin is administered by China, while India claims it as part of Ladakh.

And the India-China military boundary in this region is known as the Line of Actual Control, or LAC.

There's also the Shaksgam Valley, which Pakistan transferred to China under a 1963 agreement, a transfer India does not recognize.

So in this one region, three countries have competing territorial claims and military positions.

That's what makes Jammu and Kashmir one of the most strategically sensitive regions in the world.
