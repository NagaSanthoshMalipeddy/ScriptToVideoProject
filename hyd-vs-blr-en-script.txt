Hyderabad versus Bengaluru. Which city is better? Let's compare them!

In terms of population, Bengaluru has around 14 million people, while Hyderabad has around 11.3 million.

In terms of area, Bengaluru covers roughly 741 square kilometers, while Hyderabad is around 650.

When it comes to IT exports, Bengaluru is the clear giant, contributing a huge share of India's IT exports.

But Hyderabad isn't far behind. Its IT sector has been growing rapidly and is becoming a major competitor.

Now, startups! Bengaluru is home to 40 plus unicorns, with companies like Swiggy, Zepto and Flipkart strongly connected to the city.

But Hyderabad has built a strong reputation in space tech and deep tech, with companies like Skyroot Aerospace and Dhruva Space.

And then there's cost of living. Bengaluru generally has higher rents and deposits, while Hyderabad is often considered more affordable.

Traffic is another big difference. Bengaluru's traffic congestion is legendary, while Hyderabad benefits from wider and more planned roads.
