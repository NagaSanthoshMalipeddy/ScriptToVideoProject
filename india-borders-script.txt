Did you know India shares land borders with seven countries?

Can you guess which country has the longest border with India?

India-Pakistan border, around 3,323 kilometres.

India-Nepal border, around 1,751 kilometres, stretching along the Himalayas.

India-Bhutan border, around 699 kilometres.

And then comes the longest one...

India-Bangladesh border, around 4,096 kilometres!

India-China border, around 3,488 kilometres.

India-Afghanistan border, around 106 kilometres, making it the shortest land border.

And finally, India-Myanmar border, around 1,643 kilometres, passing through forests and hills.

But India's borders don't stop at land.

India also has a massive coastline.

India's total coastline is around 7,516 kilometres.
