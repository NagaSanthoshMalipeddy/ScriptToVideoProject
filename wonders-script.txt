What are the seven wonders of the world, and which countries are they in? Let's find out!

Number 7. Chichen Itza, Mexico. This incredible pyramid was built by the ancient Maya civilization. It's a symbol of their advanced mathematics, astronomy, and architecture.

Number 6. Machu Picchu, Peru. A hidden city built high in the mountains. Its incredible design and engineering still amaze people even today.

Number 5. Christ the Redeemer, Brazil. Standing on a mountain overlooking Rio de Janeiro, this 98-foot-tall statue has become a powerful symbol of peace and faith.

Number 4. The Colosseum, Italy. This nearly 2,000-year-old stadium is one of Rome's most iconic landmarks, famous for its incredible Roman architecture and gladiator battles.

Number 3. Petra, Jordan. A city carved directly into rock, hidden in the middle of the desert. Petra once served as an important hub for trade and culture.

Number 2. The Taj Mahal, India. Built with stunning white marble as a symbol of love, the Taj Mahal is one of the most iconic monuments in the world.

Number 1. The Great Wall of China, China. Stretching for thousands of kilometers, the Great Wall was built over centuries to protect Chinese territories from invading forces.
